Fall River Country Club

Fall River, Massachusetts

Project History

Originally opened in 1895, Fall River Country Club is a timeless New England private golf club steeped in history. Situated along the banks of the Taunton River, the club features water views on 17 of its 18 holes, along with beautiful vistas from its full-service Clubhouse.

The holes now known as the "Old Nine" were considered one of the best layouts in New England by both Bobby Jones and Francis Ouimet, who played exhibition matches here alongside Harry Vardon. In 2022, the club hired Robert McNeil, ASGCA to present a master plan of improvements, beginning a new chapter in the club's storied history.
