Fall River Country Club — Golf Course Enhancement Plan

In 2022, Fall River Country Club engaged Robert McNeil, ASGCA to develop a comprehensive Golf Course Enhancement Plan for its historic 18-hole layout situated along the banks of the Taunton River. The plan addresses all 18 holes with detailed recommendations for bunkering, drainage, green complexes, tree management, cart paths, and the short game practice area — designed to honor the club's storied 1895 heritage while elevating the playing experience for generations to come.
