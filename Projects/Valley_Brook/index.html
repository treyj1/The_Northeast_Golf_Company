A Framework for the Next Chapter

Valley Brook today is a fundamentally sound golf course on a fine piece of ground. The bones of the 1999–2001 Bill Boswell redesign remain intact, the routing flows well, and the property continues to offer the variety of holes and shot values that a public course of this caliber should provide.

The recommendations in this plan are a coordinated program of refinements — tree management to restore airflow and turf health, bunker work to renew the strategic interest and visual character of the course, tee adjustments to reintroduce yardage flexibility, and targeted short-game and circulation improvements throughout the property. Taken together, they describe a course that is healthier, more visually coherent, more strategically engaging, and better positioned to serve Bergen County for the decades ahead.

A Foundational Element

Hole 13 & Pascack Brook

Hole 13 sits on the lowest ground on the property, where the playing corridor meets Pascack Brook. Heavy rainfall overtops the banks and floods the fairway and green, while ongoing erosion steadily narrows the corridor. Addressing the flooding and bank erosion here is a foundational element of the plan.
