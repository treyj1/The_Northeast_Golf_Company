Enhancement Plan

In 2024, Haverhill Country Club engaged Robert McNeil, ASGCA to develop a comprehensive Golf Course Enhancement Plan for its historic layout at 58 Brickett Lane in Haverhill, Massachusetts. The plan provides detailed recommendations across all 18 holes — addressing bunkering, green complexes, tree management, drainage, cart paths, and practice facilities — designed to honor the club's legacy while elevating strategy and playing conditions for years to come.
