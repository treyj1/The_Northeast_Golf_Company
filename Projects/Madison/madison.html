Madison, New Jersey

Design Vision

Established in 1896, Madison Golf Club is a private 9-hole club set in the historic Victorian terrain of Madison, New Jersey. Robert McNeil, ASGCA developed a suite of design concepts exploring improvements to bunkering, green complexes, routing, and course character — positioning the club to thrive for another century while preserving its intimate, historic identity.
